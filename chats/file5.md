## 5. English public copy

### Page title
About Japanese Reading Club

### Short descriptor
Read together. Exchange languages and ideas.

### Introduction
Japanese Reading Club (JPR Club) began in Jordan on September 11, 2024, as an independent initiative by Japanese-language learners. Its starting idea was simple: make reading an enjoyable way to develop language skills and understand texts, without the pressure of traditional study.

Today, the club brings together Japanese learners and people who enjoy reading, and welcomes Japanese participants interested in language and cultural exchange. Japanese is our starting point, and we also read and discuss material in Arabic and English through in-person and online sessions.

### Section: What happens here

#### Read and understand together
We select material suited to participants—from graded readers and manga to short stories, novels, and news—and explore its meaning, vocabulary, and language points together.

#### Exchange ideas
We ask questions, share impressions, and explore different perspectives in a supportive setting. You do not need to understand every word before joining a discussion.

#### Keep the learning accessible
Our Reading Studio brings together session summaries, photographs, and notes so participants can revisit what we explored and keep learning.

### Section: What guides us
- Small, consistent steps can create a larger impact.
- Asking questions and participating open possibilities for learning and connection.
- Ideas do not need to be perfect before being shared; they improve through experience and feedback.

### Section: Explore the club
- Upcoming sessions: see what we are reading and how to participate.
- Reading Studio: revisit what we have read and discussed.
- Annual reports: explore more detailed documentation of our activities and journey.

### Section: Connect with us
Join the WhatsApp group to participate and follow club updates. You can also explore our Instagram posts, visit the YouTube channel, and listen to the podcast on Spotify.

Buttons:
- Join the WhatsApp group
- Follow on Instagram
- Watch on YouTube
- Listen on Spotify
