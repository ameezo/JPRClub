# Product Requirements

## Overview
An independent multilingual reading community founded by Japanese learners. Participants include local readers, Japanese-language learners, and Japanese visitors/students learning Arabic.

The club reads and discusses Arabic, English, and Japanese material: graded readers, manga, short stories, novels, and news.

## Multilingual Support
* English is the default interface.
* Arabic is the alternative interface.
* User-written articles stay in their original language in both versions.
* **No automatic content translation** is used.

## Core Features (Approved Future Structure)
1. **Sessions**
2. **Reading Studio** (organized by session)
3. **Resources**
4. **Journal**
5. **About**
6. **Annual reports**
7. **Private management area** (Shared)

## Publishing Rules
* Normal members request verification.
* Verified members may draft and submit content, but cannot publish.
* An administrator or authorized organizer approves publication.
* Administrators can verify members and assign/remove organizers.
* There are no appointed organizers initially.
* Must include safeguards for concurrent edits and duplicate creation in the plan.
* **Note:** Management and authentication are out of scope for the initial task.

## Out of Scope
* Reading placement tests
* Payments / Paid memberships
* Sentence analyzer
* Standalone photos-only route
* Implementing management or authentication for the prototype.
